This is Kai Yaron's H2 English Portfolio for the unit: A 'Novel' Problem. The Portfolio uses a reading theme inspired by The Duncton Chronicles by William Horwood. The reading theme is made to look and feel like an old, well-used book, while having some elements that fit the setting of The Duncton Chronicles (e.g. links, highlights).
